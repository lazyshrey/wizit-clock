# Wizit v1.0.0 — Release Notes

**Wizit** brings the iconic Apple Lock Screen Clock to your macOS desktop with native, border-free optical liquid glass.

---

### ✨ What's New in v1.0.0

- 💧 **Native Liquid Glass Optics**: Characters act as physical glass prisms that refract, magnify, and bend wallpaper background pixels with dynamic caustics, specular rim highlights, and ambient drop shadows.
- 🎨 **Custom Color Picker & Curated Tints**: Native macOS `ColorPicker` for choosing any custom tint color, alongside curated presets (*Clear Diamond*, *Ice Blue*, *Emerald*, *Sunset*, *Rose*, and *Obsidian*).
- ⏱️ **Lock Screen Typography**: Powered by Apple's native `.SFRoundedNumeric` font with independent digit animations and a bold lock-screen date header.
- 🕒 **Clean 24-Hour & 12-Hour Modes**: In 24-hour mode, hours display cleanly without leading zeros (`1:34` instead of `01:34`).
- 🎯 **Single Corner Resize Notch (`III`)**: Intuitive unified dragger at the bottom-right corner—drag horizontally for overall scale, vertically for height stretch.
- 📌 **State & Position Persistence**: Automatically saves desktop position, scale, and height aspect ratio across reboots and app restarts.
- 🪟 **Desktop Level Pinning**: Sits quietly behind all open windows (`normalWindow - 1`) with zero status bar or menu bar clutter.
- 🚀 **Launch at Startup**: Native macOS login item integration via `SMAppService.mainApp`.
- 📦 **Standalone DMG Installer**: One-step drag-and-drop installer bundle (`Wizit.dmg`).

---

### 📥 Installation

1. Download **`Wizit.dmg`**.
2. Open the disk image and drag **`Wizit.app`** into your **`Applications`** folder.
3. Launch **Wizit**. Right-click the clock anytime to access settings, lock/unlock position, or toggle startup options.
